## CORE ELEMENTS OF SYSTEM DESIGN

- Translating BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS

- Designing API and architecture 

- Understanding technology and trade-offs

## Differences between Staff and Senior Engineer

- Senior will take a problem, break it down. I saw it before, here is how we design it. (pattern regonition)

- Staff: FIRST AND FOREMOST ASK "What are we ACTUALLY TRYING TO SOLVE HERE?"

What is the RELATIONSHIP between me or my team or my organization TO the business? 

How do I apply the relationship to the thing I needed to solve?

The ability to NOT LOOK AT THE CODE, AND REASONING IN YOUR HEAD  --- IS A SKILLS

- Good system design is REALLY BORING.

## STRATEGY

1. SCOPE THE PROBLEM (10-15m)

2. DESIGN THE HIGH-LEVEL ARCHITECTURE (20-30m)

3. Address KEY CHALLENGES AND TRADEOFFS (10-15m)

# UNDERSTANDING THE PROBLEM

- DO NOT MAKE ASSUMPTION

- WHAT ARE THE REQUIREMENTS? 

- in system design interview: showoff your strengths, working through ambiguility

- it will be very vague prompt during the interview, writing everything down.

## FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

Describe WHAT the system SHOULD DO

## NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

Describe HOW the system should do.

- non functional is more challenging ones. you have to know the right questions to ask to get there.


## FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

- WHAT are the core features? -- eg: what should users be able to do?

- WHO are the users? -- eg: Are there different types of users?

- HOW do users interact with the system? -- eg: What devices are SUPPORTED?

- Are there EDGE CASES to consider?

- WHAT are the constraints?
